# Switch

A persistent on/off setting with switch semantics and a minimum 24px hit target. Use [Toggle](/Toggle) for an icon toggle button.

<SwitchDemo />

```tsx
<Switch label="Notifications" checked={enabled} onCheckedChange={setEnabled} />
```

`label` supplies the accessible name. `checked` is controlled. Disabled switches remain focusable and announce `aria-disabled`, but cannot toggle. Forced colors and reduced motion are supported.
