# Desktop component laboratory

Real compositions built entirely from public Aidos components. Use the theme toggle in the navigation, then try keyboard navigation and a narrow window.

[Try the embedded workspace example](/Workspace#interactive-example): browse five files, reorder document tabs, compare changes, and leave review notes. The inspector sits beside the work when wide and takes over when narrow; drafts, checklists, and reading positions stay in place.

## Focus

- Tab highlights the control you can operate, not the surrounding pane. Aidos focus indicators are neutral; ordinary button clicks do not add a ring.
- Text fields retain focus feedback after a click; their action buttons own their own rings.
- Tooltips never move focus. Native controls retain browser focus defaults.

## Composer

An auto-growing field and footer share one surface. The field uses a quiet 1px neutral outline; footer actions own their individual indicators. Enter submits, Shift+Enter adds a line, and IME composition does not submit. Loading or clearing a draft must resize immediately.

## Menus and placement

Try Arrow keys, Home, End, Escape, and Tab. Disabled items remain reachable and announced, but cannot activate. Focus Edit to show its tooltip: Escape dismisses the hint before the menu. The second menu prefers above/end alignment but flips and stays within the viewport. Clicking the field while a menu is open must leave focus in the field.

## Dialogs

The input receives initial focus. Try a menu inside the dialog: Escape closes the menu first, then the dialog. Closing the dialog returns focus to its trigger.

## Search palettes

Search keeps focus in its input and announces the active option. Arrow keys or Ctrl+J/K navigate; Ctrl+Home/End jump; Enter selects. Disabled entries remain reachable and announced, but cannot be selected. The same search list works without a modal.

## Document tabs

Arrow keys select tabs; Home/End jump. Delete or a separate close button closes a tab and focuses its neighbor. Long titles truncate and the tab strip scrolls horizontally without moving the page.

## Disclosure and contextual actions

Collapse the project, including via the external button. Closing content becomes inert immediately and unmounts after its animation. Reduced-motion preferences remove the transition. Hover or focus a row to reveal its actions; touch devices show them continuously.
