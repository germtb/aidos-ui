# Pane

A non-scrolling header and footer around independently scrolling content. No surface tint or reading-pane focus ring.

```tsx
<Pane
  header={<Toolbar variant="pane" headline="Review" actions={actions} />}
  footer={<Composer value={draft} onValueChange={setDraft} onSubmit={submit} />}
>
  {discussion}
</Pane>
```

- Fills a bounded flex column; use inside `Workspace` or `AdaptiveSplitView`.
- Body padding defaults to `"xlarge"`. Use `padding="none"` for an editor or nested panes.
- `scroll={false}` lets nested panes own scrolling.
- `ref` and `onScroll` target the **body**; other view attributes and `jss` target the root.
- `hidden` keeps all content mounted, including drafts and scroll position.
- The footer has a divider and minimum height of 28px. Padding belongs to its content.
- The body stays out of the Tab order. Interactive descendants retain their normal focus behavior.
