# Disclosure and Sublist

Controlled expansion with inert closing content, an exit transition, and reduced-motion support.

<DisclosureDemo />

```tsx
<Sublist label="Project" expanded={expanded} onExpandedChange={setExpanded}>
  <ListButtonItem size="small" indent={12} headline="README.md" onClick={openReadme} />
</Sublist>

<Disclosure expanded={expanded}>
  <TextInput aria-label="Details" />
</Disclosure>
```

- `Sublist` remains uncontrolled by default; `initialState.collapsed` is preserved.
- `expanded` and `onExpandedChange` make expansion application-controlled.
- `Disclosure` supplies only the content transition; the application supplies its trigger and `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` relationship.
- Closing content is immediately inert and hidden from assistive technology. It unmounts after the transition, or immediately when reduced motion is requested.
